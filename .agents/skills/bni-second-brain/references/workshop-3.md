# WS3: Find a testable Winning Zone

## Outcome and inputs
Produce a Strategy Card connecting company capability, customer value and alternatives.
The three-circle idea is adapted into a new evidence-based prompt chain; it does not prove
uniqueness or demand. Read WS1/WS2, source annexes and saved/uploaded business profile.
Do not require another course, named persona or lengthy generic intake.

## Internal prompt chain
**1. Three evidence columns.**
COMPANY: what the business demonstrably delivers, backed by product/operational/customer evidence.
Owner-stated strengths remain owner statements until corroborated.
CUSTOMER: a job/problem/outcome supported by statements or observed behavior; distinguish these.
ALTERNATIVES: what named comparable competitors or the status quo already provide, with date/scope.
Attach source IDs and limitations to each. Bring WS1's capacity/cash constraints forward.

**2. Candidate comparison.** Compare 2-3 propositions, fewer if evidence is thin.
- Winning candidate: company can deliver + customer values it + evidence of meaningful difference
  relative to the specifically researched alternatives.
- Crowded: company and alternatives deliver customer value; a specific reason to choose is needed.
- Capability gap: customers value it but the company lacks credible delivery evidence.
- Low relevance: a demonstrated capability with evidence of weak relevance to this audience.
Missing evidence means UNKNOWN. No competitor evidence is not uniqueness; no customer evidence
is not low relevance. These labels are practical analysis aids, not a claim that every label
is legible in the historical course photograph.

**3. Recommend a hypothesis.** Choose one target segment, one problem/job and proposed difference.
Explain comparative evidence, capacity/commercial fit, rejected alternatives, downside and the
strongest counterargument. Owner choice remains distinct from assistant recommendation.
If no candidate is supported, recommend a validation test instead of inventing a winner.

**4. Bounded promise.** Write up to three genuinely different phrasings of the selected
proposition; recommend one. Audience + benefit + reason to believe is sufficient.
Add timing, quantified result, price, guarantee or exclusivity only with supported and approved
public offer terms. Never require an invented deadline. Write a short 30-second pitch without
fictional endorsements, testimonials, customer numbers, awards or scarcity.

**5. Test.** One risky assumption, observable behavior, proposed sample/timebox, metric with
denominator, comparison if available and stop/change rule. Peer preference tests comprehension,
not actual purchasing. No outreach, advertising, price commitment or spend follows automatically.

**6. Website handoff.** Save/export a one-page Strategy Card and evidence annex. List public-safe
claims separately from unverified hypotheses, confidential sources and missing assets.
Brief: audience, visitor goal/CTA, supported offer, contact path, brand assets, tone and
qualifications. Never give website code raw private financial/customer evidence.
Use [checks](workshop-3-acceptance.md) and [handoff](handoff-contract.md).

## Output contract
Business/version/date; one audience/job; three-column evidence; candidate comparison;
recommendation/limits; bounded promise/reason to believe/pitch; validation test; owner choice
(unset until supplied); public-copy brief and excluded claims. Draft/PARTIAL remains until reviewed.
"Winning" describes a candidate, not achieved market success.
