# WS4 AI Board of Directors

## Goal and team mode
Use one real pending decision and accessible WS1–3 evidence. Run the five original
perspectives as separate native Codex agents on the same frozen evidence packet.
Initial responses remain independent until all five return. Batch under the host's
concurrency limit. No persona substitute or vote. Follow codex-runtime.md for actual
dispatch/completion records; a missing native capability makes this step BLOCKED.

## Execute internally

1. Reuse business/context and prior outputs. Identify one decision question, deadline,
   options including delay/do-nothing and a small test, known budget/capacity constraints,
   and the CEO's success criteria. Ask only the missing decision question or consequential
   constraint. If a supplied source already answers it, don't ask again.
2. Coordinator builds one concise evidence packet E1..En from accessible raw/WS1–3 sources:
   exact IDs, periods, currency, source dates/versions, actual review states, facts and limits.
   At most 1,000 words. Read back three material facts. Absent outputs stay absent. Draft
   prior results remain provisional. No default external browsing or guessed market facts.
3. Dispatch each native seat separately with no peer answers, at most 100 words/four lines: observation with citation; labelled risk
   hypothesis; missing evidence; one CEO question. Unsupported factual domains say
   "not in the data" and ask rather than fabricate. Preserve disagreements.
   - Skeptical CFO: cash timing, contribution assumptions, arithmetic, downside. No invented
     funding. Supplied-data operating remainder is not sales margin of safety.
   - Customer: actual review/service evidence, unmet needs and alternative explanations.
     No fabricated demand or claims that interview intent demonstrates sales.
   - Competitor: alternatives and competitive response as hypotheses. No invented plans.
   - Investor: capital exposure, opportunity cost, reversibility and evidence before commitment.
   - Respected Lao market elder: questions about local trust, relationships and operational
     fit. No stereotypes, invented local law or impersonation of a real named elder.
4. Run pre-mortem: "It is 12 months later and this failed." Produce three hypothetical
   mechanisms anchored in evidence, each with early signal, prevention and stop trigger.
   These are hypotheses, not predictions. Do not manufacture consensus or tally votes.
5. Design one small reversible experiment resolving the most consequential uncertainty.
   Include proposed owner, timebox, supplied budget ceiling or not set, baseline required,
   metric, proposed learning/completion threshold and stop rule. Finish before the actual
   decision deadline; if impossible show the conflict and propose a smaller test or a
   clearly unapproved extension request. Transparent scenario arithmetic and assumptions.
   Interviews/purchase intent alone are learning evidence, not actual demand.
6. After all five initial executions, independently dispatch Evidence Reviewer to verify
   citations, source scope, financial metric labels, dates and
   arithmetic. Repair unsupported claims internally. No real experiment, payment or messages.
7. Produce WS4_Checked_Output / Decision Memo automatically: decision/deadline, facts,
   assumptions, options/trade-offs, missing data, five-seat concerns, pre-mortem, experiment,
   source limits, state and human review fields. CEO recommendation stays blank until the
   learner supplies their own words. Never invent it from seat votes or AI's preference.

## Human finish line and handoff
Ask the CEO to check three material facts/source IDs, the experiment's budget/date against
the deadline, and supply their own recommendation or explicitly leave undecided. Record
only actual checks. Carry unresolved WS1–3 limits. Draft/PARTIAL remains honest even after
a decision if source completeness is missing. One-page memo plus short evidence annex.

Minimum by minute 30: all five sourced/unsupported-labelled perspectives and a memo with
CEO recommendation blank. Stretch: transparent bounded downside scenario or a tighter
test if supported by inputs. End with "เริ่ม WS5".
