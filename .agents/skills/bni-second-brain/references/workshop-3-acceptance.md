# WS3 acceptance
- Exact WS1/WS2 artifacts and their source limits read and carried forward.
- Company, customer and alternative evidence separately attributed.
- Missing dimension stays unknown; no evidence is not evidence of absence.
- Candidate zones compare a defined audience/use case and named alternatives.
- One recommendation explains trade-offs and what would disprove it.
- Promise has only supported benefit/timing/price/delivery claims.
- Owner statements/selection are distinct from recommendations and customer proof.
- Test measures behavior; peer votes are not purchase evidence.
- Website brief includes only public-safe facts/assets and excludes confidential inputs.
- Strategy Card, annex and handoff saved/exported and read back.
