# WS2 acceptance
- Audience, geography, targets and research question match this business.
- Collection mode is accurate; no invented run, dataset, charge or retrieval time.
- Current Actor schema/access/approved cost scope checked before a run.
- Receipt reports actual returned rows; zero/error is not evidence of no demand.
- Raw count = included + excluded; duplicate/blank/error/off-topic rules are visible.
- Every primary-theme count resolves to unique included IDs; n/N is reproducible.
- Praise and contrary evidence retained; sampled commenters are not assumed buyers.
- Material competitor facts include source/locator/date; not found is not absent.
- No invented market size, sales, complaint rate, willingness-to-pay or causal ROI.
- One test and WS3 handoff retain source dates, limitations and unknowns.
