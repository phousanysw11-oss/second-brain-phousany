# WS2 Market X-Ray

Read the [method](../bni-second-brain/references/workshop-2.md), [checks](../bni-second-brain/references/workshop-2-acceptance.md),
[Apify contract](../bni-second-brain/references/apify-collection.md) and [runtime contract](../bni-second-brain/references/codex-runtime.md).
Use WS1/profile to frame one audience, use case and decision. Prepare a bounded collection plan.
Run Apify only after actual access, Actor input and approved cost scope are checked. No account
means use supplied exports/public pages, accurately labelled; Plus does not prove Apify entitlement.
Normalize and deduplicate real records, reconcile exclusions, retain praise and contrary evidence.
Compare customer problems, valued benefits, actual competing offers and the status quo.
Counts describe the sample; missing competitor evidence does not prove uniqueness or market demand.
Deliver Market X-Ray, evidence table, actual collection receipt and WS3 handoff. An unrun plan is PLAN_ONLY.
