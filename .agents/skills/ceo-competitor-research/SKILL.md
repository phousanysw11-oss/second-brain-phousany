# Competitor research

Read [the shared local runtime contract](../bni-second-brain/references/codex-runtime.md) before work.
Reuse `data/state.json` and the selected company's accessible files; save outputs under `work/ceo/`.

Resolve the actual competitor/alternative and customer use case from saved context or one question.
Use supplied competitor files first; browse only when needed/available and retain exact source URL and retrieval date.
Separate observed claims, analyst inference and business implication. Vendor claims are not independently verified outcomes.
Compare relevant offer, price scope/currency/date, channel, proof and limits. Absence from a page means not found, not absent in reality.
No fabricated market share, traffic, customer counts, plans or local laws. SEO/market metrics stay unavailable without a real source.
Deliver a compact dossier and comparison against the status quo, with evidence gaps and what would change the recommendation.
Local sources suffice; Firecrawl/DataForSEO/SEO connectors are optional, never installation prerequisites.
Adapted from Corey Haines competitor-profiling, pinned in docs/CEO_SOURCES.json.
