# Sales support

Read [the shared local runtime contract](../bni-second-brain/references/codex-runtime.md) before work.
Reuse `data/state.json` and the selected company's accessible files; save outputs under `work/ceo/`.

Identify audience, selling situation, actual offer terms and requested asset using saved company evidence.
Use customer language, proven benefits, approved price/terms and dated competitor facts. Missing proof remains a hypothesis.
For an objection: acknowledge the concern, ask a useful discovery question, give one defensible response and cite supporting proof.
Include poor-fit conditions and avoid pressuring a buyer when the evidence shows a real fit gap.
For a one-page offer: buyer problem, offer scope, proof, approved terms and next step. No invented discount, testimonial or result.
Save a usable draft with private source notes separate from buyer-facing copy. Do not send or publish.
Adapted from Corey Haines sales-enablement, pinned in docs/CEO_SOURCES.json; CRM and partner tools are optional.
