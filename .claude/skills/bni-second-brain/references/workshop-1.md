# WS1 Business X-Ray

## Goal and inputs
Produce a one-page Business X-Ray from the learner's own accessible sales, expense,
stock and cash evidence. This is the first workshop after setup; WS2 is not required.
Use up to three months where supplied. Accept Excel/CSV, clear photos or pasted tables.
Identify the business, period and currency from actual sources. Ask only if these are
missing or conflict. Do not ask the learner to retype usable uploaded data.

## Execute internally

1. Inspect file schemas and map observed columns to sales, net revenue, matched COGS,
   expenses, stock, cash and obligations. Show three source rows with IDs and calculated
   control totals. Detect duplicate transactions/totals, subtotal rows, returns, missing
   cells, tax/discount definitions, currency/period mismatches and COGS-versus-purchases.
   Classify each X-Ray section as supported, partial or unavailable. Pause for one
   ambiguity only when it changes an affected calculation; continue other sections.
2. Calculate supported monthly net revenue, matched COGS, gross margin and supplied-data
   operating remainder. Cite source IDs and show formulas. Independently recompute sums.
   Reconcile to a separate supplied accounting total when available. Without that control,
   say completeness is unverified even if arithmetic matches the uploaded rows.
3. Rank observed products/services and separately identifiable customers by net revenue.
   Top 20% = ceil(0.2*N) observed distinct entities. Show N, selected count, ranking period,
   selected revenue, total comparable revenue, share, exclusions and tie handling. Do not
   force an 80% result. Customer ranking requires stable deidentified IDs. Blank retail
   IDs are unidentifiable, not one customer; show identifiable-customer revenue coverage.
4. Identify up to three supported margin observations. Separate observed movements from
   explanation hypotheses. Gross margin = (net revenue - matched COGS) / net revenue;
   zero revenue means undefined, missing matched COGS means unavailable. Purchases are
   not COGS. Never double-count stock writeoffs already included in expenses. Expense
   subtraction is only a supplied-data operating remainder, not certified net profit.
5. Read the dated cash snapshot and dated obligations window. Keep receivables separate
   from available cash. Show cash less known payments due as a snapshot coverage gap;
   optionally show a labelled collection scenario with its explicit timing assumption.
   Missing cash, inflows or obligations stay unknown. Do not imply a complete forecast or
   assume receivables arrive on time. Compare like periods/currencies only; a conversion
   requires an owner-supplied exchange rate and date.
6. Give three precise questions for tomorrow's team meeting, linked to evidence, gaps
   and the learner's supplied goals. Suggested causes/owners/actions remain proposals.
7. Produce WS1_Checked_Output automatically. Do not wait for a separate formatting
   prompt. Ask for the human checks below after showing the finished Draft/PARTIAL.

## Output
Business X-Ray, one page plus a compact evidence annex if needed:
- Business, scope/period/currency and source versions.
- Revenue concentration with product and identifiable-customer coverage.
- Margin observations and calculation limits.
- Cash warning, labelled scenario, or unavailable.
- Three sourced team questions.
- FACTS / ASSUMPTIONS / UNKNOWNS and source list.
- State: Draft or PARTIAL. Checked by / Date / Corrections / CEO action blank unless supplied.

## Human finish line
Ask the learner to compare three source rows, one revenue/COGS total, and the dated cash
and payments against their own files. Record only the actual check report. Carry all
limitations into WS2. Explain that matching arithmetic does not prove accounting completeness.

Minimum by minute 30: supported revenue concentration plus three team questions, missing
margin/cash clearly marked. Stretch: compare like months and investigate one observed
margin issue. End with the natural next command: "เริ่ม WS2".
