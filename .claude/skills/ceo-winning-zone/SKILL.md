# WS3 Winning Zone

Read the [method](../bni-second-brain/references/workshop-3.md), [checks](../bni-second-brain/references/workshop-3-acceptance.md)
and [runtime contract](../bni-second-brain/references/codex-runtime.md). Read actual WS1/WS2 reports and their evidence limits.
Compare demonstrated company strengths, customer statements/behavior and researched alternatives.
Mark missing dimensions unknown. Recommend one candidate with a counterargument and a disconfirming test.
A Winning Zone is a hypothesis until customer behavior and delivery evidence support it.
Produce a one-page Strategy Card: audience, job/problem, strengths, comparison scope, proposed difference,
bounded promise, reason to believe, exclusions, test and public-safe website brief.
Price/timing/guarantee claims require evidence. Separate owner selection from assistant recommendation.
Send only approved-public facts/assets to WS4, never private financial or customer records.
